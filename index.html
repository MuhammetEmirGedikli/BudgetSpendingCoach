Project explanation

About Project

A simple student presentation page for the application idea.

Project Purpose

Budget & Spending Coach is a simple web application designed to help students and young adults manage their personal finances. The app allows users to add income and expenses, organize expenses by category, calculate total income and spending, and view monthly budget summaries.

Target Users

The main users are university students and young adults who manage a limited monthly budget and need a clear view of where their money goes.

Problem Identification

Many students have difficulty managing their money effectively. They often spend without tracking where their money goes. This can lead to poor budgeting decisions. A simple and visual budgeting tool can help students understand their spending habits better.

Interdisciplinary Aspect

This project combines Computer Science with Economics and Personal Finance. The Computer Science part includes creating forms, calculations, data storage, dashboard design, and user interaction. The Finance part includes budgeting, expense tracking, spending categories, and monthly financial analysis.
